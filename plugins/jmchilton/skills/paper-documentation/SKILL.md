# Paper documentation

Work from the study's actual data, analysis, and cited sources. First identify the target venue or style requirements if they are supplied.

- Record the source for each important result, number, sample size, and statistical claim before writing it into prose. Keep observations separate from interpretations.
- Make Methods reproducible at the level the paper requires: inputs, procedure, software or instrument versions where relevant, parameters, and analysis choices.
- State Results in the same units and terms used by the underlying output. Do not imply causation, generality, or certainty beyond the evidence.
- Make abstracts and figure captions consistent with the main text. Explain what each figure shows, including groups, units, sample sizes, and tests when needed.
- Verify bibliographic details against the publication or a reliable bibliographic record. Do not invent references or silently replace uncertain citations.

Before delivery, check cross-section consistency, unresolved citations, unsupported claims, and the venue's limits. List evidence gaps that need the author's decision.
